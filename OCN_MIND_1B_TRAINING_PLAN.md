# OCN Mind 1B Training Plan

## 1. Training Routes
- **Route A (From Scratch)**: Random initialization, high data requirement.
- **Route B (Fine-tuning)**: Adapting existing models like Llama-1B or Phi-1B.
- **Route C (Continued Pretraining)**: Domain adaptation on OCN specific telemetry.
- **Route D (Hybrid Training)**: Modular training of different heads with a frozen encoder.

## 2. Comparison Metrics
- Data requirement, training time, hardware cost, final accuracy, generalization.
