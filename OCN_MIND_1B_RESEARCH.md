# OCN Mind 1B Research Document

## 1. Overview
This document outlines the research objectives for OCN Mind 1B, a specialized intelligent scheduling model for the OpenCompute Network (OCN).

## 2. Research Objectives
- Compare Dense Transformer, MoE, Hybrid, and Non-Transformer architectures.
- Evaluate training strategies: From Scratch, Fine-tuning, Continued Pretraining, and Hybrid Training.
- Optimize for task understanding, resource prediction, node matching, and scheduling policy.

## 3. Architecture Comparison Matrix
Detailed analysis of FLOPs, RAM/VRAM usage, latency, and accuracy across different hardware profiles (CPU, GPU, Mobile).
